Meet Jeremy Langdon

Jeremy is studying to receive a Bachelors of Science in Visual Communications Design at San Francisco State University with an emphasis in User Experience. He is currently working in UX/UI at Tech Fleet and at companies including Philly Truce, a community-based violence-reduction nonprofit in Philadelphia, and The Foundation for Intentional Community, a national nonprofit supporting intentional communities and social impact. He is currently progressing his education in data science. His work includes UX design, research, product strategy, and data science.
