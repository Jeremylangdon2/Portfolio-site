Meet Jeremy Langdon

Jeremy lives in the San Francisco Bay Area. He has worked as a UX designer, researcher, and product strategist for various non-profits. His current role is in UX research. He is a senior at San Francisco State University graduating with a B.S in Visual Communications Design, and is pursuing a graduate degree in data science to further his journey in data. Jeremy has also worked as an agile coach for a number of projects, supporting team growth through structured training, facilitated workshops, and guided learning on how to be agile and self-organize.
